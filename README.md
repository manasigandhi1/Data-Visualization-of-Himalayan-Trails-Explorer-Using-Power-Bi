🏔️ Trek Revenue & Customer Insights Analysis
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes trekking business performance across multiple regions, trek types, and difficulty levels.
The goal is to uncover key revenue drivers, customer behavior patterns, and operational insights to improve profitability and decision-making.

[![IMAGE ALT TEXT HERE](https://img.youtube.com/vi/FQjph8ldfcc/0.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQjph8ldfcc)

🎯 Business Problem / Objective:

  The trekking company observed fluctuations in revenue and customer engagement across different treks and seasons.We need to understand which treks are truly driving our revenue and bookings, and identify underperforming ones so we can focus our marketing and improve overall business performance. 

This project answers:
  Which treks are driving the highest revenue?
  How do difficulty levels impact customer participation?
  What seasonal trends affect bookings?
  Are high-revenue treks aligned with customer satisfaction?

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
  Power BI – Dashboard creation & visualization
  Excel / CSV – Data storage and preprocessing

  📈 Dashboard / Visualization

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive overview of business performance:

  KPI Cards: Total Revenue, Total Trekkers, Avg Treks per Customer, Avg Revenue per Trekker
  Trek-wise Revenue Chart: Identifies top-performing treks
  Difficulty Level Distribution (Pie Chart): Shows customer preferences
  Seasonal Trend Line Chart: Highlights quarterly demand patterns
  Revenue vs Rating Chart: Compares profitability with customer satisfaction
  Detailed Table View: Revenue, bookings, and performance score by trek

The dashboard enables stakeholders to filter by:
  Year
  Region
  Difficulty Level

💡 Business Recommendations
  Focus marketing efforts on top-performing treks (Goechala, Roopkund) to maximize revenue
  Introduce targeted campaigns to improve performance of mid-tier treks like Brahmatal and Kedarkantha
  Expand offerings in Moderate and Easy categories to match customer demand
  Optimize pricing or premium experiences for Difficult treks to increase participation
  Plan inventory and staffing for Q3 peak season to avoid missed opportunities
  Maintain high customer satisfaction while exploring upselling opportunities
