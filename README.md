# 🏔️ Trek Revenue & Customer Insights Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze trekking business performance across multiple regions, trek categories, and difficulty levels.

The dashboard helps identify high-revenue treks, customer participation patterns, seasonal booking trends, and overall business performance to support data-driven decision-making.

The project focuses on understanding customer preferences and optimizing trek offerings for improved profitability and operational planning.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem / Objective

The trekking company observed fluctuations in revenue and customer engagement across different treks and seasons.

The business needed to identify:

- Which treks generate the highest revenue?
- How do difficulty levels impact customer participation?
- What seasonal patterns influence bookings?
- Are top-performing treks aligned with customer satisfaction?
- Which treks require improved marketing or operational focus?

This dashboard was designed to provide a clear view of trekking business performance and support strategic planning.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Goechala and Roopkund are the highest revenue-generating treks
- Moderate and Easy treks attract the largest share of customer participation
- Difficult treks generate comparatively lower bookings despite premium pricing
- Booking activity increases significantly during the Q3 season
- Some high-revenue treks also maintain strong customer ratings, indicating balanced performance
- Certain mid-tier treks show moderate bookings but lower revenue contribution
- Revenue contribution varies noticeably across regions and trek categories
- Average revenue per trekker highlights opportunities for premium package optimization

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing campaigns on top-performing treks such as Goechala and Roopkund
- Improve visibility and promotional strategies for mid-performing treks like Brahmatal and Kedarkantha
- Expand Moderate and Easy trek offerings to align with customer demand
- Introduce premium experiences or pricing strategies for Difficult treks to improve profitability
- Prepare staffing and operational resources for peak Q3 demand periods
- Use customer ratings to identify treks requiring service or experience improvements
- Explore upselling opportunities for highly rated treks with strong repeat demand
